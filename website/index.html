DiveSend

DiveSend allows you to send dive logs from any Bluetooth (BLE) enabled dive computer and send it to your SSI dive log. No more manual entry.

Download on the App Store

Direct from your computer

Connects to your dive computer over Bluetooth and reads your dives directly — nothing routes through a third-party cloud service.

Straight to SSI

Sign in with your SSI account and send dives right into your Logbook, matched to your certifications.

Your data stays yours

Dive logs are stored on your device. DiveSend has no server of its own, no analytics, and no ads.

Why DiveSend exists

SSI's own app already syncs dives automatically from a growing list of supported dive computers. DiveSend exists for the rest of us: SSI divers whose dive computer isn't on that list yet, who'd otherwise have to type every dive into their Logbook by hand.

Because it works by reading raw data off a wide range of Bluetooth-enabled dive computers rather than a single supported model, results can vary — see below.

DiveSend isn't a dive log app itself — it just gets your dives from your dive computer into your SSI Logbook. Also, DiveSend only supports SSI!

No warranty

DiveSend is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind — always spot-check an imported dive against your dive computer before relying on it.

DiveSend reads the data your dive computer already recorded, transforms it as best it can into the format SSI's Logbook expects, and sends it along. It doesn't invent or verify dive data beyond what your computer reports.

I've directly verified this process against a single device, the Shearwater Teric. Other Bluetooth-enabled dive computers should work in the same way in theory, but haven't all been individually tested, and there could be bugs, gaps, or inaccuracies in how a given model's data is read or converted. I've not tested stuff like multi-gas setups, Nitrox, GPS, etc. In theory it should all work.

A personal project

I wrote DiveSend for myself, because I want to sync up to the SSI app as quickly as possible and have as much data as my dive computer can give. But it isn't currently supported. I'm sharing it in case it's useful to other divers in the same spot.

Feel free to email me if you have issues or want to give feedback. Just keep in mind that although I'm a professional developer, this is a hobby project I maintain in my spare time, not a full time gig.

Open source, under the hood

DiveSend wouldn't exist without a couple of great open source projects:

  • libdivecomputer (via libdc-swift), which does the heavy lifting of talking to dive computers from dozens of manufacturers.
  • ZIPFoundation, used to package up raw dive data for export.
